This acquisition

Read this before reading the picture. Depth here is not measured. It is the rate at which a pixel's image-registration vector oscillates as the satellite's look angle sweeps through the acquisition, relabelled as a distance through a declared sound wavelength that no measurement in the method constrains. Because the steering wavenumbers are evenly spaced, the volume is exactly periodic: below the marked plane every feature is a copy of one above it. Switch between a pyramid and open plateau and the volumes look alike, which is the finding. The viewer is deliberately generic, so a new site is a new data bundle rather than new code; surveyed chambers will be added as a comparison layer once their placement in the radar image is settled to better than a chamber's width.
