## 1. The noise floor: traced, and read the right way round

`snr_per_cell` divides the site's median σ⁰ measured in the image (−5.3 dB over 10 m cells, calibration factor times
mean power times the sine of the local incidence, as ICEYE defines β⁰ and σ⁰) by the specified best floor: 12.7 dB
nominal, 9.7 dB at the specified worst, 18 dB for ground at 0 dB. The darkest cells (−11.2 dB) bound the noise from
above and the ratio from below (5.9 dB), which is the correct direction and is said so. Every oracle row in P2-25,
P2-27, P2-29, P2-30, P2-32, P2-36 and P2-38 moves with it, and the single-image layer does not. Two small things:

- The specification's values are scene-centre values; the Giza site sits near the centre of a swath whose incidence

  pass when it comes) print its own.

## 3. The claim (P2-38): right as far as it goes; the shafts are the half readers will ask about

Verified: the deep void (80 m, representative) at 1,220 m as Eshelby's equal-volume sphere through Okada's point source
(the finite-size correction is of order (40/1,220)², negligible), loaded by the regional microseisms with each of 24
directions carrying 1/24 of the power; the line-of-sight imprint peaks at 1.9 × 10⁻¹² m over 0.45 km², which is the
0.36 of the bench room's peak the old scaling had, now computed; any single-image reader at most 3.6 × 10⁻⁶ above
chance, the oracle 7.2 × 10⁻⁶ at 12.7 dB, margins 2.3 × 10⁵ and 1.8 × 10⁵, still 5.2 × 10³ and 4.1 × 10³ after a
cube's moment at 1.5 and the ×10 and ×3 allowances; Khafre's measured footprint (−4.4 dB, 1.24 times the site median)
holds 10% of the imprint and moves the oracle by 1%. Three points:

- **The cube allowance is supported, not only assumed.** The fourth-review tail work compared the solver's cube with
  the analytic point source for the bench room and found the solver's field 1.22 to 1.36 times the sphere's beyond
  20 m. Cite that beside the 1.5 so it reads as a measured ratio with headroom.
- **"Strains the ground to that depth almost as at the surface"** is an upper bound, not an estimate: a Rayleigh wave's
  strain at 1.2 km under a 15 km wavelength is about half the surface value. Say "at most as at the surface"; the
  direction is conservative for the exclusion.
- **The eight shafts.** The announcement's shafts reach up to the pyramid's base and are not computed; the deep void
  is the claim's least detectable part, and a reader will see that the half with shallow tops has been left out. Their
  surface imprint is dominated by their top hundred metres, which the solver can do statically on a 300 m box
  (P2-28's loading, as P2-32 did for a shaft 4 to 30 m down), under the microseisms and, where traffic reaches, the
  lorry. One shaft would complete the claim; until then the finding should say in its first sentence that the shafts
  are not computed, not in its last.
