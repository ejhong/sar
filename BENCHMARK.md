# One auditable comparison: what one image could tell of presence, location and shape

Written 30 September 2026 at the request of two independent reviews, which asked for one fully specified comparison
before any further parameter ranges: the same source in every world, a valid reference for each world, the observation
and its tails declared, the scattering and noise model stated, bounds for presence, horizontal location and shape, and
implemented detectors' achieved performance beside the bounds, with what each detector is told recorded. The code is
`sim/experiments/p2_36_benchmark.py` (run `P2-36`); its frozen parameters are the dict `FROZEN` at the top of that file,
hashed into every result. Results are generated by the run into `sim/results/p2_36_benchmark/` (`summary.json`, and
`report.md`, the tables below filled in); nothing here is edited by hand after a run.

## What it answers, and what it does not

- **Presence**: is there a cavity? Two pairs: no cavity against a room; no cavity against an L-shaped tunnel.
- **Horizontal location**: where is it? One pair: the same room 6 m apart. By Le Cam's two-point argument any
  estimator's mean horizontal error over the pair is at least 3 m × (1 − TV).
- **Shape, depth allowed to be uncertain**: which shape? One pair at a common depth: the room against the L tunnel. A
  shape test that does not know the depth is told less than one that does, so the same-depth pair bounds it
  (TV between two mixtures over a common depth prior is at most the prior's average of the same-depth pairs); the bound
  here covers the depth computed (the shallowest, most favourable), not every depth.
- **Not answered**: absolute depth; relative vertical geometry (every layout is single-level).

## The worlds differ only in the cavity

The one-chamber bench's limestone (Mokattam: vp 3,300, vs 1,830 m/s, 2,400 kg/m³), flat ground, homogeneous, no
attenuation (the solver has none), air-filled cavities:

| world | cavity |
|---|---|
| W0 | none |
| WA | a room 6 m on a side, roof 5 m down |
| WB | an L-shaped tunnel 2.5 m square, arms 18 m and 17 m, roof 5 m down |
| WA6 | the room of WA, 6 m east |

Everything else is identical across the worlds of a pair: the exterior, the source and its timing, the scatterers (the
same speckle realisation), the receiver noise's law, the microseisms' realisation.

## Two excitations, one quiet and one favourable

- **Quiet**: Giza's regional microseisms (vertical rms 4.9 × 10⁻⁸ m/s at 0.1–0.3 Hz, measured at Kottamya 67 km east),
  as Rayleigh waves from 24 directions with independent phases, each carrying 1/24 of the power; each world's imprint is
  the solver settling under uniform horizontal strain (P2-28's loading, P2-31's layouts at this depth). The layers
  below take the **expected** squared motion over the field's realisations (an explicitly ensemble-averaged
  statement), not a peak with a 3σ allowance: a peak allowance is not a hard bound, and the expectation is what a
  measured rms level supports. The worst realisation's cost is stated beside it (√(2n) in amplitude, fourth review).
- **Strong (favourable)**: one vertical point force 15 m west of the site, the same force in every world, solved by the
  elastic solver on a box 200 × 200 × 103 m (1 m grid, 20 m absorbing layer), the surface's velocity recorded within
  ±70 m for 0.5 s (the field's energy in the last tenth of the record is under 10⁻⁶ of the whole). Its amplitude is set
  at each frequency so the vertical velocity amplitude at the site without a cavity is √2 times the FTA's rms level
  for a truck over a bump at 15 m (72 VdB), and that harmonic is held for the whole 24.5 s pass: generous by
  construction (the bump is a transient; the vibration's power is put at one frequency). The frequency rule, fixed
  before the bounds were computed: the frequency in the FTA band (8–100 Hz) at which the presence (room) pair's oracle
  on the measured disc is largest. Every frequency of the band is also reported.

## The observation is the whole image; the tail beyond the measured disc is an assumed envelope

The image is the Giza 2025 dwell's (113,692 rows 4.4 cm apart along track, 11,562 columns 0.43 m apart in ground
range: 5 km square), the site at its centre. Within 70 m of the site the solver's field is used as computed; beyond
it an envelope, labelled assumed:

- strong case, the cavity's scattered difference: energy per unit area ≤ A(θ)·(70 m / r) with A(θ) the largest
  r-weighted value in each 10° azimuth bin of the 55–70 m ring (and its neighbours'): a surface wave carried to the
  image's edge without attenuation. The measured energy per metre of radius σ(r) on 5 m annuli from 10 to 70 m is
  reported as the test of that assumption (flat for an unattenuated surface wave, falling for body waves);
- strong case, the lorry's own wave (in each world's reference): the same form about the source, from its 40–55 m ring;
- quiet case: |u|² ≤ A(θ)(58 m / r)^2.8 beyond 58 m (P2-28's receivers reach 60 m), the slowest decay measured on a
  100 m domain (fourth review, A3), empirical.

The results are shown with the tail multiplied by 2 and 5, with illustrative attenuation (Q of 50 and 20 at the
rock's Rayleigh speed), with the grid allowance (P2-26's largest amplitude change on halving the grid, 11%, empirical),
and restricted to the measured disc and to 39 m.

## Scattering and noise: fully developed speckle, white receiver noise at an assumed SNR

White circular Gaussian scatterers on the image's pixel grid (texture power one per cell), white receiver noise at SNR
per cell; the image model's Doppler-to-time relation (checked pulse by pulse in P2-25: the model's Fisher information
within 1% of the pulse-exact one at 20 and 60 Hz, 2% at 150 Hz). **SNR 30 dB is assumed**: σ⁰ about 0 dB for the
brightest natural ground over ICEYE Dwell's best specified NESZ (−26.7 dB, product specification); the acquisition's
own calibration and noise records are not in this repository. Every result is swept over 20–60 dB. Bright persistent
points break the speckle model; they are covered by the oracle only.

## The layers, and what each is told

| layer | told | not told | kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1, per-line certificate | the excitation's realisation, both worlds' whole motion, the noise level | the texture (speckle) | proved upper bound within the model, any method reading the one image |
| L2, oracle (ensemble) | as L1, and every scatterer's reflectivity | which world; the noise's realisation | proved upper bound on the average over scenes |
| D1, oracle's test on fixed scenes | as L2 | as L2 | achieved: exact for each fixed scene, pulse by pulse |
| D2, score test | the differential pattern and its time history | the texture | achieved: its weak-signal deflection from the exact Fisher information on the real image's band within 70 m, and the statistic implemented on synthesised images of a patch (24 grounds, at the real level and amplified 800 times) |
| D3, the published method | nothing about the cavity | everything | achieved, read blind: P2-31 for the quiet case at amplified levels; not run on the strong case here, where the single-image certificate bounds it |

An oracle's bound that fails (TV below 0.9) is a strong exclusion: a detector told less does no better. An oracle's
bound that succeeds shows only that the information is there for a privileged detector.

## The equations

States, one rule for every layer: detection rate ≤ false-alarm rate + TV at every operating point; **near chance** if
TV < 0.05, **95% found at 5% false alarms excluded** if 0.05 ≤ TV < 0.9, **unresolved** if TV ≥ 0.9.

**L1.** Widen the image's range band to every range bin (the real image is a function of the widened one, so this can
only add information); then each along-track line is independent of the others (white scatterers, white noise, the
motion a function of each scatterer's own place and time), and KL adds over lines. On one line of n pixels with n_b
in-band azimuth bins, the in-band covariance is C = B Bᴴ + σ²I with B = E ∘ exp(−iφ), E the in-band rows of the
unitary DFT (orthonormal) and φ(x, t(ν)) = 4π d(x, t)/λ at the bin's stationary time. Theorem C per line:

- q² = Q / λ_min(C₀), Q = ‖B₁ − B₀‖²_F = Σ_x (n_b / n) ⟨4 sin²((φ₁ − φ₀)/2)⟩ (the differential motion);
- λ_min(C₀) ≥ σ² + (1 − ‖B₀ − E‖_F)², ‖B₀ − E‖²_F = Σ_x (n_b / n) ⟨4 sin²(φ₀/2)⟩ (world 0's whole motion on that
  line: the lorry's wave, world 0's own cavity, the microseisms' envelope 7.2 × 10⁻⁸ m);
- KL_line ≤ ρ² / (2(1 − ρ)), ρ = 2q + q²; KL = Σ_lines; TV ≤ min(√(KL/2), √(1 − e^(−KL))).

With a harmonic, ⟨4 sin²(δ/2)⟩ ≤ (k₀|Δ|)²/2 · (1 + 1/(2π f T)) for every phase ψ (T the pass). The whole-image floor of
earlier runs failed under strong shaking because the common wave's phase energy summed over 1.3 billion pixels
exceeds one; on one line it is of order 10⁻⁴, and the floor stays near one.

**L2.** E Δ² = 8 Σ_j SNR_j ⟨sin²(k₀ δ_j / 2)⟩ ≤ snr · cells · k₀² (1 + 1/(2π f T)) ∫ |Δ|² dA, snr the SNR per cell
over the processed azimuth band's share, cells = 28.1 per m²; TV ≤ 2Φ(√(E Δ²)/2) − 1 (Jensen). The common motion
cancels in each scatterer's difference; it does not cancel in a fixed scene's coherent sum, where cross terms remain:
D1 computes them.

**D1.** For a fixed scene the oracle's statistic is Gaussian in the receiver noise and its ROC is exactly
Φ(Φ⁻¹(α) + Δ). The run computes |μ₁ − μ₀|² pulse by pulse (P2-29's echo model, complex128) on patches at full density
where the pair differs most, both worlds' whole motion on every scatterer, with the common wave as simulated and ten
times larger, against the ensemble formula.

**Verification of L1.** On one line of the model (2,048 px, cyclic) through the largest difference, the exact KL
between CN(0, C₁) and CN(0, C₀), each world's whole motion and the noise in it, from the eigenvalues of the whitened
change, against the line's certificate, at the real level and amplified 10, 100 and 1,000 times.

## What would change the conclusion

Each margin is the factor by which the differential motion's amplitude would have to grow before its layer stops
excluding 95% at 5%. A proponent's allowances (a louder local level, site amplification, a closer or louder source,
higher SNR for the oracle, a slower-decaying tail) multiply into that factor; the run shows the states under the
tabulated allowances and prints the margins. For the oracle, the SNR at which it reaches 0.9 is printed: a calibrated
noise floor for the real product would replace the assumed 30 dB.
