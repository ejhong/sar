# One auditable comparison: what one image could tell of presence, location and shape

Written 30 September 2026 at the request of two independent reviews, which asked for one fully specified comparison
before any further parameter ranges: the same source in every world, a valid reference for each world, the observation
and its tails declared, the scattering and noise model stated, bounds for presence, horizontal location and shape, and
implemented detectors' achieved performance beside the bounds, with what each detector is told recorded. The code is
`sim/experiments/p2_36_benchmark.py` (run `P2-36`); its frozen parameters are the dict `FROZEN` at the top of that file,
hashed into every result. Results are generated by the run into `sim/results/p2_36_benchmark/` (`summary.json`, and
`report.md`, the tables below filled in); nothing here is edited by hand after a run.

**Revision 3 (1 October 2026, after the eighth review).** Only the analysis changes: the worlds, the lorry, the solver,
the geometry and the frequency are those of revision 2 (their hash, `WORLDS_HASH`, is unchanged, so the solver's fields
and P2-37's images carry over). Revision 2 is preserved at commit `afee349` (`BENCHMARK.md`, `p2_36_benchmark.py` and
its `summary.json`); its errors are listed in the erratum below.

**Revision 2 (30 September 2026, after the sixth review: the external reviewer's five comments and a second review,
BENCHMARK_REVIEW.md on another branch).** A fifth world, the 10 m room the earlier runs left open; the quiet case
averaged over the excitation's realisations through a bound linear in each line's phase energy, with the microseisms'
worst-case envelope in every reference; the score test run with one noise law and a threshold set on separate grounds;
the envelope beyond 70 m stated as a declared assumption, not a proved enclosure; the oracle's crossing stated as where
its bound stops excluding, not where a detector succeeds.

### Erratum to revision 2

1. **The noise floor was misread.** Revision 2 called an earlier −26.7 dB noise floor untraceable and took ICEYE's
   documentation 6.0.0 (Dwell and Dwell Fine, −18 to −15 dB) as the calibration, giving 12.7 dB. In documentation 6.0.8,
   Table 2-11 gives Dwell −26.7 to −15.6 dB and Dwell Fine −23.7 to −12.6 dB at scene centre; the 2025 product (SLEDF)
   is Dwell Fine. The −26.7 dB was Dwell's figure misapplied to a Dwell Fine product, not an untraceable one; and no
   specified figure is a measurement of this acquisition's noise. Revision 3 keeps every specified value as a scenario
   and takes the most favourable for the mode as the headline (18.4 dB), the conservative choice for an exclusion.
2. **The score test's evaluation grounds were calibration grounds.** Calibration used seeds 3600–3799 and the null
   evaluation 3700–3759, so every null evaluation image had set the threshold, and the reported 5% false-alarm rate
   was not an independent measurement. Revision 3 draws named, disjoint seed streams (asserted), 400 calibration
   grounds and 100 + 100 evaluation grounds, and reports each achieved rate with its interval.
3. **Four verification rows were stale.** The exact-divergence check on one line was cached under a version string
   that did not include the noise level; four worlds' rows came from the earlier 30 dB configuration (the floor they
   report implies it), the fifth from 12.7 dB. Every analysis cache is now keyed on its inputs as well as its version
   (`katabasis.runs.memo`, `inputs=`), and the rows are recomputed.
4. **The worst realisation's pass mean.** The quiet case's worst-realisation phase energy took the pass mean of cos² as
   one half, which is exact only on average over the phase; over a finite pass it is at most (1 + 1/(ωT))/2. Revision 3
   uses that bound (the strong case already did).
5. **The covariance floor's fallback.** Where a line's common motion leaves the Weyl term no positive floor, the receiver
   noise still floors the covariance: λ_min ≥ σ² + [max(0, 1 − √Q_c)]². `sarsim.finite` now uses the positive part
   (it had returned no floor); no revision-2 number depended on it, P2-38's allowances do.

## What it answers, and what it does not

- **Presence**: is there a cavity? Three pairs: no cavity against a 6 m room, an L-shaped tunnel, a 10 m room.
- **Horizontal location**: where is it? One pair: the same room 6 m apart. By Le Cam's two-point argument any
  estimator's mean horizontal error over the pair is at least 3 m × (1 − TV).
- **Shape, depth allowed to be uncertain**: which shape? One pair at a common depth: the room against the L tunnel. A
  shape test that does not know the depth is told less than one that does, so the same-depth pair bounds it
  (TV between two mixtures over a common depth prior is at most the prior's average of the same-depth pairs); the bound
  here covers the depth computed (the shallowest, most favourable), not every depth.
- **Not answered**: absolute depth; relative vertical geometry (every layout is single-level).

## The worlds differ only in the cavity

The one-chamber bench's limestone (Mokattam: vp 3,300, vs 1,830 m/s, 2,400 kg/m³), flat ground, homogeneous, no
attenuation (the solver has none), air-filled cavities:

| world | cavity |
|---|---|
| W0 | none |
| WA | a room 6 m on a side, roof 5 m down |
| WB | an L-shaped tunnel 2.5 m square, arms 18 m and 17 m, roof 5 m down |
| WA6 | the room of WA, 6 m east |
| WC | a room 10 m on a side, roof 5 m down (revision 2: the case P2-22, P2-26, P2-29 and P2-30 left open, there under a plane wave) |

Everything else is identical across the worlds of a pair: the exterior, the source and its timing, the scatterers (the
same speckle realisation), the receiver noise's law, the microseisms' realisation.

## Two excitations, one quiet and one favourable

- **Quiet**: Giza's regional microseisms (vertical rms 4.9 × 10⁻⁸ m/s at 0.1–0.3 Hz, measured at Kottamya 67 km east),
  as Rayleigh waves from 24 directions with independent phases, each carrying 1/24 of the power; each world's imprint is
  the solver settling under uniform horizontal strain (P2-28's loading, P2-31's layouts at this depth). The result is
  an average over the field's realisations, reached through a bound that is linear in each line's phase energy for
  every realisation (below), not a certificate evaluated at the average energy (the certificate is convex, so that
  would bound nothing); the worst realisation's own certificate is stated beside it, and no 3σ peak allowance is used.
- **Strong (favourable)**: one vertical point force 15 m west of the site, the same force in every world, solved by the
  elastic solver on a box 200 × 200 × 103 m (1 m grid, 20 m absorbing layer), the surface's velocity recorded within
  ±70 m for 0.5 s (the field's energy in the last tenth of the record is under 10⁻⁶ of the whole). Its amplitude is set
  at each frequency so the vertical velocity amplitude at the site without a cavity is √2 times the FTA's rms level
  for a truck over a bump at 15 m (72 VdB), and that harmonic is held for the whole 24.5 s pass: generous by
  construction (the bump is a transient; the vibration's power is put at one frequency). The frequency rule, fixed
  before the bounds were computed: the frequency in the FTA band (8–100 Hz) at which the presence (room) pair's oracle
  on the measured disc is largest. The band is also reported every 2 Hz: its largest value is the largest sampled,
  not a proved maximum between samples.

## The observation is the whole image; the tail beyond the measured disc is an assumed envelope

The image is the Giza 2025 dwell's (113,692 rows 4.4 cm apart along track, 11,562 columns 0.43 m apart in ground
range: 5 km square), the site at its centre. Within 70 m of the site the solver's field is used as computed; beyond
it an envelope, labelled assumed:

- strong case, the cavity's scattered difference: energy per unit area ≤ A(θ)·(70 m / r) with A(θ) the largest
  r-weighted value in each 10° azimuth bin of the 55–70 m ring (and its neighbours'): a surface wave carried to the
  image's edge without attenuation. The measured energy per metre of radius σ(r) on 5 m annuli from 10 to 70 m is
  reported beside it: it falls between 40 and 70 m, which supports the extrapolation but does not prove that it
  encloses the field to kilometres;
- strong case, the lorry's own wave (in each world's reference): the same form about the source, from its 40–55 m ring;
- quiet case: |u|² ≤ A(θ)(58 m / r)^2.8 beyond 58 m (P2-28's receivers reach 60 m), the slowest decay measured on a
  100 m domain (fourth review, A3), empirical.

Beyond 70 m this envelope supplies 97 to 98% of the strong case's signal, so the computed field, the assumed envelope
and the sensitivity tests are kept apart: the results are given within the measured 70 m, with the envelope, with it
multiplied by 2 and 5 (robustness tests, not its verification), with attenuation at Q of 50 (a realistic rock) and 20,
with the grid allowance (P2-26's largest amplitude change on halving the grid, 11%, empirical), and within 39 m.

## Scattering and noise: fully developed speckle, white receiver noise at a specified SNR

White circular Gaussian scatterers on the image's pixel grid (texture power one per cell), white receiver noise at SNR
per cell; the image model's Doppler-to-time relation (checked pulse by pulse in P2-25: the model's Fisher information
within 1% of the pulse-exact one at 20 and 60 Hz, 2% at 150 Hz). **The SNR is conditional** (`sarsim.radiometry`):
measured backscatter over a specified noise floor. The site's median σ⁰ is measured in the image, −5.3 dB over 10 m
cells; the noise is not (the product carries no noise field), so it is ICEYE's specification for the product's mode,
Dwell Fine, which differs between versions of the documentation (Table 2-11): −18 to −15 dB in 6.0.0, −23.7 to
−12.6 dB in 6.0.8, scene-centre values (the site lies near the centre of a swath whose incidence runs 20.6° to 21.0°).
The headline is the best of these, **18.4 dB**, the most favourable to a detector; the sweep holds every other
specified value (12.7, 9.7, 7.3 dB), ground at 0 dB over the best floor (23.7 dB), 30 and 40 dB for bright persistent
ground, and the measured lower bound (5.9 dB: the darkest cells, −11.2 dB, bound the noise from above and so the ratio
from below). The single-image layer barely depends on the SNR (the noise enters only its floor); the oracle's does.
Bright persistent points break the speckle model; they are covered by the oracle only, and at 40 dB its bound no longer
excludes: that is the one open corner of the strong case.

## The layers, and what each is told

| layer | told | not told | kind |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1, per-line certificate | the excitation's realisation, both worlds' whole motion, the noise level | the texture (speckle) | proved upper bound within the model, any method reading the one image |
| L2, oracle (ensemble) | as L1, and every scatterer's reflectivity | which world; the noise's realisation | proved upper bound on the average over scenes |
| D1, oracle's test on fixed scenes | as L2 | as L2 | achieved: exact for each fixed scene, pulse by pulse |
| D2, score test | the differential pattern and its time history | the texture | predicted: its weak-signal deflection from the exact Fisher information on the real image's band within 70 m; achieved: the statistic implemented on synthesised images of a patch, one noise law, the 5% threshold set on 400 calibration grounds and the performance read on 100 + 100 grounds disjoint from them (seed streams asserted disjoint), each rate with its Clopper–Pearson interval |
| D3, the published method | nothing about the cavity | everything | achieved, read blind: P2-31 for the quiet case at amplified levels; P2-37 on the benchmark's strong-case images, with a positive control |

An oracle's bound that excludes (TV below 0.9) is a strong exclusion: a detector told less does no better. Where the
oracle's upper bound stops excluding, this argument no longer excludes the target; it does not show that any detector
reaches it (the bound includes the assumed envelope, and the fixed-scene checks cover small patches, not whole-scene
recovery). A second image of the same ground is not equivalent to knowing every scatterer's reflectivity, and partial
reference information is not bounded here.

## The equations

States, one rule for every layer: detection rate ≤ false-alarm rate + TV at every operating point; **near chance** if
TV < 0.05, **95% found at 5% false alarms excluded** if 0.05 ≤ TV < 0.9, **unresolved** if TV ≥ 0.9.

**L1.** Widen the image's range band to every range bin (the real image is a function of the widened one, so this can
only add information); then each along-track line is independent of the others (white scatterers, white noise, the
motion a function of each scatterer's own place and time), and KL adds over lines. On one line of n pixels with n_b
in-band azimuth bins, the in-band covariance is C = B Bᴴ + σ²I with B = E ∘ exp(−iφ), E the in-band rows of the
unitary DFT (orthonormal) and φ(x, t(ν)) = 4π d(x, t)/λ at the bin's stationary time. Theorem C per line:

- q² = Q / λ_min(C₀), Q = ‖B₁ − B₀‖²_F = Σ_x (n_b / n) ⟨4 sin²((φ₁ − φ₀)/2)⟩ (the differential motion);
- λ_min(C₀) ≥ σ² + (1 − ‖B₀ − E‖_F)², ‖B₀ − E‖²_F = Σ_x (n_b / n) ⟨4 sin²(φ₀/2)⟩ (world 0's whole motion on that
  line: the lorry's wave, world 0's own cavity at its worst realisation, and the microseisms' envelope for every
  realisation of the 24 directions, √24 × 7.2 × 10⁻⁸ m);
- KL_line ≤ ρ² / (2(1 − ρ)), ρ = 2q + q²; KL = Σ_lines; TV ≤ min(√(KL/2), √(1 − e^(−KL))).

With a harmonic, ⟨4 sin²(δ/2)⟩ ≤ (k₀|Δ|)²/2 · (1 + 1/(2π f T)) for every phase ψ (T the pass). The whole-image floor of
earlier runs failed under strong shaking because the common wave's phase energy summed over 1.3 billion pixels
exceeds one; on one line it is of order 10⁻⁴, and the floor stays near one.

**The quiet case, averaged.** For every realisation of the 24-wave field, each line's phase energy is at most Q_max
(the directions' amplitudes added in phase) and its common motion within the worst-case envelope, so with
q_max = √(Q_max/λ) and ρ_max = q_max(2 + q_max), KL_line ≤ c·Q/λ with c = (2 + q_max)²/(2(1 − ρ_max)): linear in Q.
Averaging over realisations, E KL ≤ Σ c·E[Q]/λ, and E TV ≤ min(√(E KL/2), √(1 − e^(−E KL))) since both are concave.

**L2.** E Δ² = 8 Σ_j SNR_j ⟨sin²(k₀ δ_j / 2)⟩ ≤ snr · cells · k₀² (1 + 1/(2π f T)) ∫ |Δ|² dA, snr the SNR per cell
over the processed azimuth band's share, cells = 28.1 per m²; TV ≤ 2Φ(√(E Δ²)/2) − 1 (Jensen). The common motion
cancels in each scatterer's difference; it does not cancel in a fixed scene's coherent sum, where cross terms remain:
D1 computes them.

**D1.** For a fixed scene the oracle's statistic is Gaussian in the receiver noise and its ROC is exactly
Φ(Φ⁻¹(α) + Δ). The run computes |μ₁ − μ₀|² pulse by pulse (P2-29's echo model, complex128) on patches at full density
where the pair differs most, both worlds' whole motion on every scatterer, with the common wave as simulated and ten
times larger, against the ensemble formula.

**Verification of L1.** On one line of the model (2,048 px, cyclic) through the largest difference, the exact KL
between CN(0, C₁) and CN(0, C₀), each world's whole motion and the noise in it, from the eigenvalues of the whitened
change, against the line's certificate, at the real level and amplified 10, 100 and 1,000 times.

## What would change the conclusion

Each margin is the factor by which the differential motion's amplitude would have to grow before its layer stops
excluding 95% at 5%. A proponent's allowances (a louder local level, site amplification, a closer or louder source,
higher SNR for the oracle, a slower-decaying tail) multiply into that factor; the run shows the states under the
tabulated allowances and prints the margins. For the oracle, the SNR at which its bound stops excluding 95% at 5% is
printed beside the headline one.

## Scope

Five layouts at one depth, one geology (uniform limestone, no attenuation in the solver), the declared excitation, fully
developed speckle, a specified rather than measured noise floor, and the assumed envelope beyond 70 m. A shallow room is
not by itself an upper bound on every larger or deeper structure; the claimed structures under Khafre are computed on
their own (P2-38). The next case is the real acquisition: a diffuse-plus-bright-scatterer model informed by the image
itself, with Khafre's geometry; at 40 dB, which stands for bright persistent ground, the oracle's bound does not exclude.
