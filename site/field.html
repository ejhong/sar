    F03
    Measurement calibration

    Can the tracker recover a known shift?

    Known translations are applied to a 512 × 512 crop of the real complex image. The same patch tracker used in the mechanism studies measures 25 fixed, interior patches for each input. Both signs, both axes and a zero input are retained.

    Injected (row, col) pxMedian measured px95th percentile error px≤ 0.02 px?

    These translations are controlled software inputs, not measured ground vibration. Patches from one crop are dependent. Passing a 0.02 px calibration tolerance does not demonstrate sensitivity to the 0.005 px motion gate or resolve subsurface depth.

    04 / The next defensible test

    A prediction must earn
    its place in the model.

    There are currently zero validated field depth results. A chamber-shaped rendering would add no evidence until the measurement, geometry and competing explanations survive these checks.

    1. 01

      Lock the coordinates.

      Identify unambiguous surface correspondences, quantify error on unused landmarks, qualify the vertical datum and confirm the cavity’s present state.

    2. 02

      Validate the aperture.

      Tie image-frequency support to collection time for this dwell product. Test controlled phase motion and surface-only inputs through that same adapter.

    3. 03

      Save, then score.

      Freeze targets, flanks and processing. Score location, roof depth and extent against surveys and displaced maps. Keep misses, rejected outputs and false alarms.

    The first Giza comparison will be exploratory: the surveys informed the design. Later sites and independent acquisitions can provide tests withheld from tuning. A shallow-chamber success would not, by itself, validate deep columns beneath Khafre.
