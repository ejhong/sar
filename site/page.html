Can radar reveal
what lies below?

Measuring surface motion, locating a cavity and reconstructing columns are three different claims.

The proposed route infers underground geometry from surface motion. Known physical inputs, noise controls and competing explanations test when depth is recoverable and when an image can mislead.

Where the evidence stands
  1. Surface motion: measurable.

    Demonstrated on controlled satellite targets in published work; verified in our ideal measurement model.

  2. Depth: possible under tested assumptions.

    Our coupled model succeeds with sufficient signal and known excitation. Wrong assumptions can produce false detections.

  3. Khafre columns: not established here.

    Original scans are available for inspection. No buried structure or depth has been validated with them in this notebook.

Which method is actually being tested? +

The original publications, the public derivative protocol and this implementation are separate objects of study. The derivative protocol explicitly supplies choices absent from the original descriptions. These experiments cannot be called an exact replication of the original Khafre processing.

ComponentImplemented hereWhat remains conditional
RegistrationComplex patch correlation; injected-translation regression.The 32-pixel patch implementation does not reproduce an unpublished processor or every context-window refinement.
Nuisance correctionSeparate two-arc geometric medians and diagnostics in the new independent-scene controls.T1–T7 retain their declared global-median correction. The favorable moving-reflector benchmark assumes zero nuisance.
Selective branch BBest harmonic within each W25 window; gate windows separately; focus accepted windows only. No accepted window gives no output.The empirical ellipse and amplitude criteria need physical sensitivity and false-alarm validation.

05 / WHAT WOULD THE REAL SCANS ESTABLISH?

Start with chambers
whose geometry is known.

Giza already has measured shafts and burial chambers. Comparing a fixed method with those surveys is a useful first field test. A match must explain the buried geometry beyond what visible surface structures already reveal.

Real-data status · 20 Sep 2026

Original complex ICEYE scans of Giza (27 August 2025) and Sacsayhuamán (22 August 2025) passed preliminary metadata and small-sample read checks. Native-image registration is under review. The depth results above remain simulations; no surveyed chamber has yet been evaluated.

Why accurate placement comes before a depth test +

At the Giza scene centre, changing assumed surface elevation by 10 m shifts the metadata projection by about $giza_height_shift_px range pixels, equivalent to roughly $giza_height_shift_m m on flat ground. This is a geometric sensitivity calculation, not measured surface motion. It is large compared with the candidate chambers.

The HDF5 raster is 113,692 × 11,562 pixels; the companion SICD XML describes 11,558 × 113,620. A transpose cannot register the two products. The native RPC projection also has residual differences from the supplied centre and corner coordinates when one average scene height is used. Those metadata points are not independent surveyed landmarks.

Next: align survey plans with independent surface landmarks and their elevations, record the remaining placement uncertainty, and verify the aperture support needed for motion analysis. No target-specific adjustment will be fitted to make a depth result match a tomb.

Large files: what is needed and how to inspect them +

Keep files in place. The inspector reads metadata without loading the raster. An optional explicit crop has a working-memory cap. HDF5 I/Q and uncompressed complex TIFF reads are supported; SICD/NITF uses optional sarpy. Compressed TIFFs need a windowed reader.

.venv/bin/python scripts/inspect_sar.py /path/to/scene.h5

# Optional native-image QC crop; select bounds explicitly.
.venv/bin/python scripts/inspect_sar.py /path/to/scene.h5 \
  --rows 1000 1512 --cols 2000 2512 --out data/inspection

A spatial crop can truncate the support needed for sub-aperture analysis. This preview is for image and metadata QC; the processing region is chosen after inspecting the acquisition. Missing geometry is reported rather than filled with simulated defaults.

Umbra’s open-data program also supplies complex products. Available imagery alone does not supply surveyed cavity labels.

For Khafre, the decisive evidence would connect repeatable measurements to a quantitatively successful, independently validated physical model. These synthetic results establish neither the presence nor the absence of underground columns.

A short glossary +
SAR / SLC
A moving radar forms an image; a single-look complex image retains amplitude and phase.
Surface motion
A displacement that changes radar phase or image behavior. Its underground cause is a separate inference.
Model depth
A coordinate assigned by a model. Ground truth is needed to validate it.
Identifiability
Whether different physical explanations can be distinguished by the measurements.
Positive control
An input containing a known signal the method should recover.
False alarm
An accepted detection on a known negative under the complete, fixed rule.
SNR
Signal-to-noise ratio. Here it is prescribed per isolated reflector and aperture sample.
Held-out depth
A target depth excluded from the estimator’s candidate library.
Layover
Different elevations and locations overlap in radar-image coordinates.
