# Published Khafre Project images reproduced for comparison and critique

These four images were released by the Khafre Research Project (C. Malanga, F. Biondi, A. Mei, N. Ciccolo)
around the press conference of 15–16 March 2025 and circulated widely. They are reproduced here at reduced
size, unaltered, solely to compare the published output with the simulation in test T7. Copyright remains
with their authors.
